Seafood Boil

Description

This seafood boil recipe is a delicious and easy way to prepare a variety of seafood in a flavorful broth. It's perfect for feeding a crowd or enjoying as a hearty meal.

Ingredients

  • Seafood (shrimp, crab, clams, mussels, etc.)
  • Potatoes
  • Corn on the cob
  • Andouille sausage
  • Old Bay seasoning
  • Lemon
  • Garlic
  • Butter
  • Salt
  • Pepper

Steps

  1. Combine all ingredients in a large pot.
  2. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  3. Reduce heat and simmer for 15-20 minutes or until the seafood is cooked through.
  4. Remove from heat and serve hot.
