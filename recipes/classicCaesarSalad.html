Classic Caesar Salad

Description

This classic Caesar salad recipe is a timeless favorite. With its crisp romaine lettuce, creamy Caesar dressing, and crunchy croutons, it's a simple yet delicious salad that's perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • Romaine lettuce
  • Caesar dressing
  • Croutons
  • Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Wash and dry the romaine lettuce.
  2. Tear the lettuce into bite-sized pieces.
  3. Combine the lettuce with the Caesar dressing and toss to coat.
  4. Sprinkle croutons and Parmesan cheese on top.
