Crispy Fried Chicken

Description

This crispy fried chicken recipe is a classic comfort food favorite. The chicken is marinated in buttermilk, coated in a seasoned flour mixture, and then deep-fried to golden perfection. Serve it with your favorite sides for a delicious meal.

Ingredients

  • Chicken pieces (drumsticks, thighs, or breasts)
  • Buttermilk
  • All-purpose flour
  • Salt
  • Pepper
  • Garlic powder
  • Onion powder
  • Paprika
  • Vegetable oil (for frying)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oil to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Pat the chicken pieces dry and season with salt and pepper.
  3. Dredge the chicken in the seasoned flour, shaking off excess.
  4. Drop the chicken into the hot oil and fry until golden brown and cooked through.
  5. Remove and drain on paper towels.
